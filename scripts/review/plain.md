## How you write

These rules apply to the summary and to every entry. They follow the numbering of the unslop skill (github.com/aaronchu415/claude-config, skills/unslop), keeping the rules a review tends to break; the numbers are stable so a rule can be cited.

7. **Plain words.** Not "crucial", "delve", "enhance", "landscape", "pivotal", "underscore". The everyday word.
8. **"Is" and "has".** Not "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "carries" for plain possession.
13. **No em dashes.** A period or a comma in their place. A parenthesis, an en dash, or a hyphen used as a dash is not a substitute.
14. **Colons only before a list or an example.** A colon joining two clauses becomes two sentences.
23. **No filler.** "In order to" is "to". "It is important to note that" is deleted.
26. **No metaphor nouns.** Not "surface", "latch", "floor", "track", "wire", "road", "home", "hostage". Say the concrete thing: the property, the rule, the file, the function.
27. **Say what it does, not how it feels.** If a sentence cannot be restated as an instruction, a fact, or a number, cut it. "Green means ship" becomes "the suite passes with the fold shut".
28. **One idea per sentence.** If a reader would backtrack, split it or drop a clause. A sentence stays under twenty words.
29. **Active voice.** Name the actor. "The tally is read from the card" becomes "the test reads the tally from the card".
31. **The plain word.** "Use", not "utilize" or "leverage". "Many", not "numerous". "If", not "in the event that".
32. **No mannered prose.** No aphorisms, no rhetorical fragments, no personified code ("the sheet listens", "the door asks", "the suite believes"), no figurative verbs ("rides", "stands on", "reaches for"). The site's pages speak that way on purpose; a review does not. Say the mechanism.
33. **Whole sentences.** No dropped articles, no arrows, no abbreviations, no symbol-speak. "8rem floor on the child, 4.4rem on the parent" becomes "the list has a minimum height of 8rem, and the rule on the nav sets 4.4rem, so the nav cannot shrink the list."
